# Florida tax deed coverage — all 67 counties

How every Florida county sells tax deeds, and how this product covers each one.
This is the evidence base behind the "all 67 Florida counties" claim: live
auction feeds for every county that sells online, and the sale schedule plus
clerk records for the counties that sell in person.

**Summary (verified 2026-08-19):**

| Coverage | Counties | What the app shows |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Online — RealAuction (`{county}.realtaxdeed.com`) | 45 | Live auction feed: every parcel, opening bid, assessed value, deep links — refreshed daily |
| In person at the courthouse | 21 | County page: how and when that county sells, the clerk's tax deed page, and the published sale list where one exists |
| Online — other platform (Okaloosa via Bid4Assets) | 1 | County page with links to the Bid4Assets auction and the clerk's records (feed adapter is future work) |

## How this was verified

- **RealAuction (45):** discovered from the platform's own county selector and
  scraped daily (`config/counties.json`, regenerated by CI discovery). Every
  county in the live feed traces to a real `{county}.realtaxdeed.com` site.
- **DeedAuction (Grant Street):** a CI audit (`.github/workflows/coverage.yml`,
  `python -m scraper coverage`) probed `{county}.deedauction.net` for all 22
  counties not on RealAuction, plus Broward. Result: **only
  broward.deedauction.net resolves** (Broward also runs on RealAuction, which
  is where our feed reads it); the other 22 hosts don't exist in DNS. The
  platform is not a source for any county we lack.
- **In-person counties (21):** each county's clerk site was researched
  individually; the sale method, schedule, and links below come from the
  clerk's own published pages and are stored in `config/florida_counties.json`
  (hand-maintained — CI never overwrites it), which the exporter embeds in the
  data feed as `counties_registry`.
- **Okaloosa:** sells online via Bid4Assets (`bid4assets.com/OkaloosaFLTax`),
  a platform the scraper doesn't parse yet. Shown honestly as online-elsewhere.

Some in-person counties run their sales through list apps (St. Johns and Levy
use TaxSmart list pages; Collier publishes an upcoming-sales app) — those are
candidates for future clerk-list parsers, which would add their parcels to the
feed without any change to this table's honesty rule: **the app never claims a
live auction feed for a county that doesn't sell online.**
